
## Limits of Binary Representation
**Determine the limits of binary representation:**
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in unsigned binary=255 (Binary: 11111111)
   - Largest positive number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement)=127 (Binary: 01111111)
   - Smallest negative number with 8 bits in signed binary (two's complement)=-128 (Binary: 10000000)
